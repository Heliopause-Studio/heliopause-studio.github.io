Timing Quest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Timing Quest is made by Heliopause Studio (Ángel Párraga Muñoz, Spain). Contact: heliopause.dev@gmail.com

What the game collects

Nothing. Timing Quest has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send any personal data to Heliopause Studio or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings and a record of your past fights are saved only on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the game.

The settings menu lets you save that fight record as a file, for example to send it to us when you report a problem. This only happens when you choose to do it, and you decide where the file goes and who receives it. If you email us a file, we use it only to improve the game and delete it on request.

Purchases

The full game is unlocked with a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We never see your payment details; the game only asks Google Play whether this Google account owns the full game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
